<p class="lead">
I am a Ph.D. candidate in the School of Artificial Intelligence & Automation at Huazhong University of Science and Technology (HUST), supervised by <a href="https://lab.bciml.cn/">Prof. Dongrui Wu</a>. My work lies at the <strong>intersection of brain–computer interfaces and machine learning</strong> for smart healthcare. I study EEG decoding under distribution shift and data scarcity, focusing on <strong>cross-species/device/modality/subject generalization</strong> and <strong>knowledge-driven brain-signal generation</strong>. Looking ahead, I am building <strong>large-scale EEG foundation models</strong> that are parameter-efficient, privacy-aware, and transferable across species, tasks, populations, and sensing modalities, with an emphasis on clinical reliability and deployment.
</p>

# Internships
- *10 / 2022 - 04 / 2023*, [Alibaba Cloud](https://www.alibabacloud.com/), China.
  - **Anomaly-aware data augmentation:** designing **five augmentation operators** leveraging **N-grams** and **TF-IDF** to augment positive-class samples, increasing data availability and improving the **generalization** of fault-detection models.
  - **Long-sequence forecasting with Transformer:** Quantified anomaly-feature importance to identify **salient/sparse queries**, and designed a **SparseAttention** module to handle long sequences more efficiently.
  - **Domain-generalized MoE architecture:** Addressed substantial temporal and device-level distribution shifts in server-fault data by designing a **domain-generalized Mixture-of-Experts** model for robust prediction under distribution shift.
  - Authorized patent (student first author).
